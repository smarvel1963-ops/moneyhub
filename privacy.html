Money Hub privacy policy

Money Hub is an app by Marvel Corp. Last updated October 22, 2026.

The short version

What you type into Money Hub stays on your phone. Money Hub never asks for a bank password and never connects to your bank. We have no Money Hub account system and no database of your money. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. A few features you choose to use send data to other services, and each one is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Your accounts and the balances you type, transactions, payments and bills, budgets, debts, subscriptions, paydays, savings goals, to-dos, lists and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. Bank, card and payment-app files you import (CSV, OFX or QFX) are read on your phone; the file is not sent anywhere. Photos in the Document Vault (receipts, bills, tax and insurance papers) are kept on your phone only and are not part of any backup. We cannot see any of it.

You can erase it any time in ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data also removes everything.

If Auto Hub or Home Hub is on the same phone, Money Hub can read their costs and bills from the phone's storage to show them in one place. That reading happens on the phone; nothing is sent. Turn it off in ⚙ → Hub family.

Google user data

Money Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in Money Hub or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Not a bank, not advice

Money Hub is a personal tracker. It does not hold money, move money, lend, or connect to any bank or card account. Its forecasts and "Can I afford it?" answers are arithmetic on the numbers you entered, not financial advice.

Children

Money Hub is for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information. We hold almost none of it.

Changes

If what Money Hub sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
