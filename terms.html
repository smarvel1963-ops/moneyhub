Using Money Hub

Money Hub is a personal money tracker for the numbers you type in. It never connects to your bank and never asks for a bank password. The free version needs no account. Money Hub is made for adults. By using it you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

Money Hub Pro

Free Money Hub keeps 3 accounts, with bills, budget, debt payoff, subscriptions and the payday planner. Money Hub Pro is a paid subscription of $2.99 a month, with a 30-day free trial. It adds unlimited accounts, importing your bank's CSV file, Reports and backup to your own Google Drive. Pro is sold and billed through Whop. Whop's own terms apply to the purchase, renewal and cancellation. Purchases are made on Whop's website, not through Google Play. The email you bought with unlocks it, on up to 3 phones at a time. A Day Hub Pro purchase also unlocks Money Hub. The AI helper and Hub AI Credits (one-time packs of 100 questions for $2.99 or 500 for $9.99, usable in any Hub app) are sold the same way, and Settings shows what your plan includes. If it ends, the paid features stop, and your data and the free features stay.

Not financial, tax or legal advice

Money Hub is a tracker. It does not hold or move money, lend, or connect to any bank or card account. Its totals, forecasts and "Can I afford it?" answers are arithmetic on the numbers you entered, not financial, tax or legal advice. Balances are what you typed, so they can be out of date. Bill reminders can be missed: phones can silence notifications and connections drop, so don't rely on Money Hub alone to pay a bill on time. AI helper answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
